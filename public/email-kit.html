Saving a template in Gmail (one time)

  1. Gmail → Settings (gear) → See all settings → Advanced tab → Templates: Enable → Save changes.
  2. Click Compose, click into the message body, and paste what you copied above.
  3. In the compose window click the three dots (bottom right) → Templates → Save draft as template → Save as new template, and give it a name.
  4. To use it later: Compose → three dots → Templates → pick the name. Templates do not add your signature twice; delete the duplicate if Gmail shows one.

Tip: paste with normal Ctrl+V / Cmd+V, not "paste without formatting". If a logo looks like a broken image, you are offline or on a draft preview; it loads from independentjourney.com.
